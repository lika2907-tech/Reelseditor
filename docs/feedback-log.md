# Reels Engine page: feedback log

Malika's feedback, kept for the next edit round. The live page is the first
redesign (commit aa96e97). No edits until the new video footage arrives.

## Direction (applies to everything)
- **Old money. High-level, rich-buyer experience.** Quiet, restrained, expensive.
  Nothing that reads as hype, gimmick or template.
- **Mobile first.** Most visitors arrive from Instagram, on a phone, inside the
  Instagram in-app browser. Design and review every section at phone width first.
  - The loader adds about 3 seconds before anything shows. Cut it on mobile or make it very short.
  - Test the pinned sections and smooth scrolling inside the Instagram browser.
  - Keep tap targets large, text readable without zooming, and the buy button
    always within reach.

## Messaging, in her words
- AI is here to expedite processes, not kill your authenticity.
- Let AI amplify your voice, but you need to find it first.
- The technology is here to support you, not replace you.
- Delegate the most boring part of creating content.
- You record in a flow, creative state, often after client calls, but you don't
  publish because writing captions and posting is too much friction.
- I built a system that automates the whole process, from editing to writing
  captions to posting. All it needs is your feedback or approval, and it publishes
  for you.
- Your most important gems shouldn't get lost in your phone, your notes and your
  camera roll.
- The goal is not to sell a technical tool. It is an entry into her ecosystem and
  teachings: meticulous, detail-oriented, three years in AI, prompt engineering.
  When people hear "Authentic AI Content System", it should blow their mind.
